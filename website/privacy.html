Privacy Policy

KatalogitAI · Last updated: August 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, store name, email, phone number, city, PIN code and optional store location.
  • Product content: the photos you upload and the catalogue data we generate for you.
  • Usage data: credits used, jobs created, and basic technical data needed to operate the Service.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and operate the Service (AI generation, catalogue storage, credit management).
  • To send essential account emails (verification, password reset) and, only with your consent, promotional messages.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

3. How we protect it

  • Passwords are never stored by us — sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication over TLS.
  • Your photos are stored in private, per-store storage; other users can never access them.
  • All traffic is encrypted, access to admin tools is restricted to authorised staff, and every sensitive operation is logged.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted processors that help us operate the Service (such as cloud infrastructure and email delivery providers), under confidentiality obligations, and with authorities when required by law.

5. Your rights

  • You can access and update your account details in Store Settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account and your data by contacting us; we will action it within a reasonable time (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • You can ask us what data we hold about you at any time.

6. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period after deletion where required for legal or anti-fraud purposes. Generated and uploaded content is deleted when your account is deleted.

7. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email hello@katalogit.ai. We respond within one business day.
