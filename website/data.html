Data Policy

KatalogitAI · Last updated: August 2026

1. What this policy covers

This Data Policy explains how your product photos, catalogue data, credit history and account activity are stored, processed and deleted on the KatalogitAI platform.

2. Your catalogue data

  • Photos you upload and the AI-generated photoshoots, descriptions and export files we create are stored in private, per-store storage. Only your account can access them.
  • Every product is owned by your store account. Other sellers never see your catalogue.
  • Draft products (saved when your credit balance is low) are kept until you recharge and generate, or delete them.

3. Credit & usage records

  • Your credit balance, top-ups and generations are recorded in a ledger for accounting and anti-fraud purposes.
  • Authorised staff may review anonymised usage to improve the Service and prevent abuse.

4. AI processing

  • Your uploaded photo is processed by our AI pipeline to generate your catalogue. We do not train public models on your photos.
  • Generated images carry the marketplace-compliance notes described in the Service; you are responsible for each marketplace's imagery rules when listing.

5. Deletion & portability

  • You can delete any product from your catalogue at any time; the photos are removed from our storage.
  • Deleting your account removes your catalogue, photos, and account data. Credit transaction records are retained in anonymised form for legal/accounting purposes.
  • You can request an export of your catalogue data by contacting us.

6. Security

Access to your data is protected by authentication, server-side ownership checks, encrypted transport, and rate-limited APIs. Suspicious activity is logged and reviewed.

7. Contact

Questions about your data: hello@katalogit.ai.
